We created a pair of asymmetric keys. What are their names? Which is public and which is private?
server-key.pem is the private key, made with openssl genpkey
server-cert.pem contains the public key, made with openssl req-x509
It's a self-signed certificate: the public key plus identity info (the questions you pressed Enter through), signed with the private key.






What is a certificate authority (CA)? What kind of attack can a CA prevent?
A CA is a third party. It checks who owns a public key and signs a certificate.
of who belongs to this server.

It prevents man-in-the middle attacks. 
AKA: the attacker sits between client and server and sends the client their own public key
while pretending to be the server. 
without a CA, the client can't tell the differences.
The CA encrypts everything to the attacker. 

So the idea is that encryption along isn't enough, you also have to know who you are encrypting to.
